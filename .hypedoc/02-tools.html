Everything a sound needs, one keystroke away

Each tool opens in its own window. Originals are never touched.

SearchCtrl F

Every word matches on its own, across names, categories and folders. "door squeak" finds Squeaky_Door_02.wav.

SlicerCtrl K

Cut one file into many. Drag regions, tap M while it plays, or auto-detect hits between silences.

LoopCtrl L

Crossfade the tail over the head so it repeats with no click. A/B against the raw loop with B.

EffectsCtrl P

Reverse, pitch, speed, fades, mono, volume, peak or LUFS normalize. Before/after waveforms and presets.

ExportCtrl E

WAV 16/24/32-bit or OGG Vorbis at any sample rate, with numbered or lettered names.

MCP Serveropt-in

Let Claude Desktop, Cursor or VS Code search, tag and export your library over localhost.
